Commutative

Change the order; the answer stays the same.

3 + 5 = 5 + 3
3 × 5 = 5 × 3

Works for + and ×, not − or ÷.

Associative

Change the grouping; the answer stays the same.

(2 + 3) + 4 = 2 + (3 + 4)
(2 × 3) × 4 = 2 × (3 × 4)

Works for + and ×, not − or ÷.

Distributive

Multiply each number inside the brackets.

3 × (4 + 2) = 3 × 4 + 3 × 2
3 × (4 − 2) = 3 × 4 − 3 × 2

Works with + or − inside brackets.

Supplementary angles

Two angles whose sum is 180°. They need not touch.

Other angle = 180° − given angle

Linear pair

Two angles sharing a vertex and an arm, with their other arms forming a straight line.

Every linear pair is supplementary; supplementary angles need not form a linear pair.

Physics

Motion

s = speed; d = distance; t = time
v = velocity; Δx = displacement

s = d ÷ t
d = s × t
t = d ÷ s
v = Δx ÷ t
Δx = v × t
t = Δx ÷ v

Use constant or average s and v.

Waves

Frequency (f): waves passing a point each second.
Wavelength (λ): distance between neighbouring crests.
Period (T): time for one complete wave.

Wave speed: v = f × λ = λ ÷ T
Frequency: f = v ÷ λ = 1 ÷ T
Wavelength: λ = v ÷ f = v × T
Period: T = 1 ÷ f
Frequency: f = number of waves ÷ time
Number of waves = f × time
Distance travelled by wave: d = v × t
Travel time: t = d ÷ v

Units: f in hertz (Hz); λ and d in m; T and t in s; v in m/s.

T = time for one wave; t = total time.
Wavelength is one wave’s length; d is how far the wave travels.
