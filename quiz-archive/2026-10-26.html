QUESTION 21 / 23hard

Physics: A toy car travels 60 metres in 20 seconds, then another 60 metres in 30 seconds. What is its average speed for the whole journey?

Need a hint?

Add both distances and both times first. Average speed is total distance divided by total time.

QUESTION 22 / 23medium

Chemistry: In a sealed container, 12 grams of carbon reacts completely with 32 grams of oxygen to make only carbon dioxide. No material escapes. What mass of carbon dioxide forms?

Need a hint?

Atoms rearrange in a chemical reaction. In a sealed system, the total mass before and after stays the same.

QUESTION 23 / 23hard

Mathematics: A bag has 3 red marbles and 2 blue marbles. You pick one marble at random, do not put it back, then pick another. What is the probability that both are blue?

Need a hint?

The first blue has probability 2/5. After taking it out, there is just 1 blue among 4 marbles. Multiply the two probabilities.
