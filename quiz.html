04 / BONUS ROUND

Sneak peak - IIT Bonus Questions

3 original, simplified challenges · Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics. Inspired by JEE-style concepts; not past-paper questions.

QUESTION 21 / 23hard

Physics: A solid block has a mass of 54 g. When fully submerged in a measuring cylinder, it raises the water level from 40 mL to 60 mL. What is the block's density? Use 1 mL = 1 cubic centimetre.

Need a hint?

The rise in water level gives the block's volume. Density is mass divided by volume.

QUESTION 22 / 23medium

Chemistry: A neutral atom contains 9 protons and 10 neutrons. How many electrons does it contain?

Need a hint?

Protons carry positive charge and electrons carry equal negative charge. A neutral atom's charges must balance; neutrons carry no charge.

QUESTION 23 / 23hard

Mathematics: How many diagonals does a hexagon have? A diagonal joins two corners that are not next to each other.

Need a hint?

From each corner, count the non-neighbouring corners you can join. If you count from every corner, each diagonal is counted twice.
