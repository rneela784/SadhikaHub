04 / BONUS ROUND

Sneak peak - IIT Bonus Questions

3 original, simplified challenges · Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics. Inspired by JEE-style concepts; not past-paper questions.

QUESTION 21 / 23hard

Physics: A light ruler balances on a pivot. A 6-newton downward force acts 20 cm to the left of the pivot. What downward force, 40 cm to the right, balances it? Ignore the ruler's weight.

Need a hint?

For balance, force multiplied by distance from the pivot must be equal on both sides.

QUESTION 22 / 23medium

Chemistry: You dissolve 10 grams of salt completely in 90 grams of water. Nothing is lost. What percentage of the total solution's mass is salt?

Need a hint?

The solution includes both salt and water. Divide the salt's mass by the total mass, then multiply by 100.

QUESTION 23 / 23hard

Mathematics: A rectangle has a perimeter of 40 cm. Which pair of side lengths gives the greatest area?

Need a hint?

All four pairs use the same perimeter. Multiply length by width for each pair and compare.
