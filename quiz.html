3 original, simplified challenges · Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics. Inspired by JEE-style concepts; not past-paper questions.

QUESTION 21 / 23hard

Physics: A child stands 3 metres in front of a fixed plane mirror, then walks 1 metre straight towards it. What is the distance between the child and the child's image after moving?

Need a hint?

A plane mirror places the image as far behind the mirror as the object is in front. First find the child's new distance from the mirror.

QUESTION 22 / 23medium

Chemistry: Which sequence would recover dry salt from a mixture of salt and clean sand, using water and ordinary filter paper?

Need a hint?

Salt dissolves in water, but sand does not. Dissolved salt passes through ordinary filter paper with the water.

QUESTION 23 / 23hard

Mathematics: A robot must move exactly 2 squares right and 3 squares up to reach a target on a grid. It can only move right or up, one square at a time. How many different routes can it take?

Need a hint?

Each route is an order of five moves containing two R's and three U's. Count the different pairs of positions that the two R's can occupy.
